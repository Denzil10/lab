Tokendle

Five strings a day. Guess how many tokens each one costs a language model. Within 10% is green, within 35% is yellow.

Counts come from o200k_base, the open tokenizer behind OpenAI's GPT-4o-family models, computed ahead of time with tiktoken by build_pool.py. Claude and Gemini use their own tokenizers, so their counts differ. Nothing you type leaves this page.
